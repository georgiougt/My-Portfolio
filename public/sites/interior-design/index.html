How a project runs

Most homes take five to nine months. You will always know which stage you are in and what it costs.

  1. 01

    Listen

    We walk the space together and talk about how you live, what you own and what you would never part with.

    2 weeks
  2. 02

    Concept

    Layouts, a material palette and reference images presented in the studio with real samples on the table.

    4 weeks
  3. 03

    Detail

    Drawings, lighting plans, joinery and a line-by-line schedule your builder can price without guessing.

    8 weeks
  4. 04

    Deliver

    We manage orders, visit site weekly and install everything down to the books on the shelves.

    To completion
