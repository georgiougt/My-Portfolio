The house

We cook over oak and vine cuttings, serve what the week brings, and pour wine from growers we know by first name.

Aurel opened in a former tannery with one hearth, forty seats and a short list of rules. Nothing travels far. Nothing is rushed. The menu changes when the market does, sometimes twice in a week.

Come for the tasting menu at the long table, or take a stool at the bar and order as you go.

Chef and founder

A good fire does half the work. The rest is knowing when to leave things alone.

Matteo Rinaldi

Matteo trained in Piedmont and spent a decade on the line in Copenhagen and London before building the hearth at Aurel by hand. He still lights it himself each afternoon at three.
