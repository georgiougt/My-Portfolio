The idea

A bathhouse is not a luxury. It is an old, simple habit: get warm, get cold, rest, repeat. We built a place to practise it properly.

Heat

Cedar sauna, steam room and a heated stone bench to lie on.

Water

Three pools at 38, 32 and 12 degrees, filled from our own spring.

Touch

Massage and facial work from therapists with a decade of practice.

The thermal circuit

Warm, cold, rest, repeat

  1. 01 · 15 minutes

    Warm

    Start in the sauna or steam room until you feel properly hot.

  2. 02 · 1 minute

    Cool

    Step into the cold plunge. Breathe out slowly. It passes.

  3. 03 · 20 minutes

    Rest

    Lie down in the garden room with tea and nothing to do.
