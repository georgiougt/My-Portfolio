Hello

I photograph weddings the way I would want mine remembered.

That means I spend the day in the background, close enough to catch your grandmother's face during the speeches and far enough that you forget I am there. You get around six hundred finished photographs, a handful of which will make you cry in a good way.

I take twenty weddings a year, so the person you email is the person who turns up.
