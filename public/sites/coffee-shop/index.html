Our craft

From cherry to cup.

  1. 01

    Source

    We cup hundreds of samples each harvest and buy the handful that stop the table talking.

  2. 02

    Roast

    Twelve kilos at a time on a restored drum roaster, profiled by hand and by taste.

  3. 03

    Rest

    Five to ten days of rest lets the coffee settle before it meets a grinder.

  4. 04

    Brew

    Recipes are dialled in every morning. Water, dose, time and temperature, written on the board.
