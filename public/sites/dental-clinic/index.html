Good to know

Do you take my insurance?

We work with most major plans and submit the claim for you. Bring your card and we will confirm cover before treatment.

I have not been in years. Is that a problem?

Not at all, and you will not get a lecture. We start with a conversation and go at your pace.

Can I spread the cost?

Yes. Treatments over 300 can be paid in interest-free instalments across six or twelve months.

How soon can I be seen?

Usually within the week. Emergencies are seen the same day when you call before ten.
