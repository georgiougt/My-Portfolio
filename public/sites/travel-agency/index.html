Our approach

Three steps, no templates

You will work with one designer from the first call to the day you come home.

  1. 01

    We listen

    An unhurried call about how you like to travel, what you want to feel, and what you would rather avoid.

  2. 02

    We draw the route

    A day-by-day proposal with places to stay, people to meet and room to wander. We refine it until it is yours.

  3. 03

    You simply go

    Everything is booked and confirmed. Your designer and our local hosts are a message away, day and night.

Nine days, lake to summit

The quiet Dolomites

Arrive by water

A private boat across the lake to a family-run hotel on the shore. Mornings on the water, afternoons in the orchards, and a first dinner cooked by your host's mother.

  • Lakeside suite, three nights
  • Dawn row with a local boatbuilder
  • Cellar visit and tasting

Into the high valleys

Walk hut to hut with a mountain guide while your bags travel ahead. The paths are old shepherd routes, and lunch is whatever the hut is cooking that day.

  • Two nights in a restored rifugio
  • Guided ridge walk, at your pace
  • Evening with a wood carver

Above the clouds

Finish in a glass-fronted lodge at two thousand metres. Take the sunrise cable car, then do nothing at all in the spa while the weather moves through the peaks.

  • Summit lodge, three nights
  • Sunrise ascent and breakfast
  • Private transfer to Venice
